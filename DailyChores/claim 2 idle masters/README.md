# ⏱️ claim 2 idle masters

## What it does

Runs **Idle Master** **two times** in a row to push through daily quest progress efficiently.

## Why twice?

The loop is tuned around **Perfect Smash** pacing in **Void Vortex** and similar fast-resolve content — one pass is often not enough to fully **smash** all relevant **daily quest** credit for your setup. Running it **twice** aligns with how many players clear those bars without manual babysitting.

## Your setup matters

**Configure Idle Master in-game however you prefer** (which modes it visits, battle count, etc.). This macro only triggers the Idle Master flow — it does not choose your teams or routes inside the feature.

Recommended before first unattended run:

- Enable **Skip Battle** where applicable.
- Confirm Idle Master won’t route into content your account can’t clear.

## Macro file

[`claim 2 idle masters.json`](./claim%202%20idle%20masters.json)

> ⚠️ Macros can sometimes fail if Idle Master opens an unexpected screen, combat doesn’t skip, or a popup interrupts the sequence.
